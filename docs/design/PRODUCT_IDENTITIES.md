## Application

- Rail / section eyebrow / focus ring may take product accent.
- Body text stays snow/fog on graphite — never long passages in accent color.
- Switching Suite products should change accent tokens without remounting brand fonts.
- Audience surfaces (Artist, Live stage) may use atmospheric bg; accent still matches table.
