## What VYBZ is

VYBZ is a **premium social network**. Everyone gets **My VYBZ** and can express themselves however they want. Creation is optional. Belonging is not.

Thesis (locked): *VYBZ is a living social identity that becomes a creative operating system when you create.*

## What VYBZ is not (as the product)

These still exist in the tree. Do not treat them as current direction:

- The Station / radio as the product
- Pack suite, Pack Maker, marketplace as the product
- Living Mix / live-mix streaming platform as the product
- Creator OS / song workspace / Suite command center as the product
- Workspace as signed-in home
- DAW plug-in, studio desks, or a sample-pack pipeline as the identity of VYBZ

Hide, never delete. Routes may still resolve.
