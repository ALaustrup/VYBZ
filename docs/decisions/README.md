# Product decisions

**These files are history.** They explain why a past identity was locked. They do not decide what VYBZ is now.

**Current authority:** [`../../PRODUCT.md`](../../PRODUCT.md) Version 9 (place/vocabulary amendment 2026-08-23).

**Current identity record:** [`0011-living-profile.md`](./0011-living-profile.md).

VYBZ is a **social network**. A VYBZ is a person’s living place. Thesis in PRODUCT: *a living social identity that becomes a creative operating system when you create.* Creation is optional. Living Mix, Pack Maker, Workspace-as-home, studio desks, and the DAW plug-in are preserved capabilities — not the product.
