VYBZ is the release operating system for independent music.

Everything between finished and released: prepare your project, analyze masters, credit collaborators, package for distribution, then present your work on your artist page with VDock playback, tips, and live.

Phase 13 Android Beta brings:
• Offline-safe upload queue with retry and progress
• Deep links (vybz://release/…) into your release workspace
• Flexible in-app updates on the Play beta track
• Sealed preferences via Android KeyStore
