- **Account information:** email address and authentication credentials (managed by our authentication provider), and your chosen username/display name.

- **Usage & interaction data:** plays, reactions, ratings, connections, matches, downloads, and feature usage.

We use personal information to: (a) provide, secure, and improve the Service; (b) operate features you use (matchmaking, feed, messaging, collaboration, downloads); (c) apply provenance and anti-abuse measures (e.g., watermarking, provenance ledger, spam prevention); (d) communicate with you (notifications, service updates); and (e) comply with legal obligations.
