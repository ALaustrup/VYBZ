# Performance / Booking Agreement

**A Codex document provided by Astra Matrix, Inc.**

A performance agreement books an artist for a live show, fixing the fee and payment, set time, cancellation terms, and the technical/hospitality **rider**. Use it between an artist (or their agent) and a venue/promoter.

---

## LIVE PERFORMANCE AGREEMENT

**Effective Date:** [DATE]
**Artist:** [ARTIST / ACT NAME] ("Artist")
**Purchaser (Venue/Promoter):** [NAME] ("Purchaser")
**Venue:** [VENUE NAME & ADDRESS]
**Date & Set Time:** [SHOW DATE], set [START–END], soundcheck [TIME]

### 1. Engagement
Purchaser engages Artist to perform a set of approximately **[LENGTH]** on the date above. Artist shall perform professionally and on schedule; Purchaser shall provide the venue, stage, power, and access per this Agreement and the Rider.

### 2. Compensation
- **Guarantee:** **$[AMOUNT]**, [plus [X]% of net box-office over $[THRESHOLD] ("versus")].
- **Deposit:** **$[AMOUNT]** ([50]%) due by [DATE]; balance due **in full before performance** [in cash / by wire].
- **Withholding/Taxes:** as required by law; for foreign artists, applicable withholding and any tax-waiver process applies.

### 3. Rider
The attached **Technical Rider** (stage plot, input list, backline, monitors, sound/lights) and **Hospitality Rider** (green room, food/drink, towels, parking, comps) are incorporated and binding. Material rider items marked "essential" are conditions of performance.

### 4. Cancellation & Force Majeure
- If **Purchaser** cancels, the deposit is non-refundable and [the full guarantee is due if within [X] days].
- If **Artist** cancels (other than force majeure), Artist shall refund the deposit and use reasonable efforts to reschedule.
- Neither party is liable for failure to perform due to **force majeure** (illness, government action, severe weather, venue closure); the parties shall attempt to reschedule.

### 5. Recording / Streaming
No audio/video recording or livestream of the performance is permitted without Artist's prior written consent. Artist retains all rights in its performance, name, and likeness; Purchaser may use Artist's approved name/image solely to promote this show.

### 6. Merchandise
Artist may sell merchandise; venue merch fee: **[0–20]%**, settled after the show.

### 7. Insurance & Safety
Purchaser shall provide a safe stage and premises and maintain appropriate liability insurance.

### 8. Independent Contractor; Governing Law
Artist performs as an independent contractor. Governed by the laws of [STATE/COUNTRY]; venue [VENUE].
