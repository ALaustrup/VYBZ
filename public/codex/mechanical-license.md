# Mechanical License (Cover Song)

**A Codex document provided by Astra Matrix, Inc.**

A mechanical license lets you reproduce and distribute a **cover** of someone else's composition in audio-only formats (streams, downloads, CD, vinyl). In the U.S. this is available as a statutory/compulsory license; digital mechanicals are administered by the Mechanical Licensing Collective (MLC). This template covers a **direct** license between the parties.

Licensee may make a musical arrangement to the extent necessary to conform to its performance style, but shall not change the basic melody or fundamental character, and acquires no ownership of the underlying Composition.
