# Work-for-Hire Agreement

**A Codex document provided by Astra Matrix, Inc.**

A work-for-hire agreement engages a contributor (session musician, vocalist, engineer, designer) for a flat fee, with the resulting work owned by the hiring party. It includes an assignment backstop in case "work made for hire" does not apply by law.

---

## WORK-FOR-HIRE AGREEMENT

This Work-for-Hire Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of [DATE] between **[HIRING PARTY / COMPANY]** ("Company") and **[CONTRIBUTOR LEGAL NAME]**, p/k/a **[NAME]** ("Contributor").

### 1. Engagement & Services
Company engages Contributor to provide the following services: [describe — e.g., session bass on [TITLE]; lead vocals; mixing; cover artwork] (the "Services"), delivering the results (the "Work Product") by [DATE].

### 2. Compensation
Company shall pay Contributor a flat fee of **$[AMOUNT]**, payable [on delivery / 50% up front], as full consideration for the Services and all rights granted. Contributor is **not** entitled to royalties, publishing, or ongoing compensation unless expressly stated in **Exhibit A**.

### 3. Ownership — Work Made for Hire
The Work Product is a **work made for hire** under applicable copyright law, and Company is the author and owner of all rights. To the extent any portion is not a work made for hire, Contributor hereby **irrevocably assigns** to Company all right, title, and interest in the Work Product, including all copyrights and neighboring rights, worldwide, in perpetuity.

### 4. Moral Rights & Further Assurances
To the extent permitted by law, Contributor waives moral rights in the Work Product and agrees to execute any documents reasonably needed to perfect Company's ownership.

### 5. Credit
Contributor [shall / shall not] receive credit substantially as: "[CREDIT]".

### 6. Independent Contractor
Contributor is an independent contractor, responsible for their own taxes; nothing creates an employment or partnership relationship.

### 7. Warranties
Contributor warrants the Work Product is original or properly cleared, does not infringe third-party rights, and that Contributor is free to enter this Agreement.

### 8. Confidentiality
Contributor shall keep unreleased materials and business terms confidential.

### 9. Governing Law
Governed by the laws of [STATE/COUNTRY]; venue [VENUE].
