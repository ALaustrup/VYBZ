# Collaboration Agreement

**A Codex document provided by Astra Matrix, Inc.**

A collaboration agreement sets the ground rules between creators working together on one or more works: how decisions are made, how ownership and income are shared, how credit is given, and what happens if someone leaves.

---

## COLLABORATION AGREEMENT

This Collaboration Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE] by and among the undersigned collaborators (each a "Collaborator" and collectively the "Collaborators").

### 1. Purpose & Scope
The Collaborators will jointly create [describe: one or more musical works / an EP / a project titled "[NAME]"] (the "Works").

### 2. Ownership
Unless a per-work **Split Sheet** states otherwise, ownership of each Work's **composition** and **master** shall be shared as follows: [equally among Collaborators / as listed in Exhibit A]. Each Work's specific splits shall be confirmed in a Split Sheet upon completion.

### 3. Income & Expenses
Net income from the Works shall be divided per the applicable splits. Pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses (studio, mixing, mastering, artwork) shall be [shared pro-rata / recouped from first income] and documented.

### 4. Decision-Making
Ordinary creative and release decisions require [majority / unanimous] approval. The following require **unanimous** written approval: sync licensing, sale/assignment of rights, exclusive deals, and use of a Work in advertising.

### 5. Credit
Each Collaborator shall be credited for their contribution in metadata and liner notes.

### 6. Departure of a Collaborator
A Collaborator may withdraw from future work on written notice. A withdrawing Collaborator **retains** their vested ownership and income share in Works already created but relinquishes rights in Works created after departure. No Collaborator may block the exploitation of a completed Work absent a Section 4 approval right.

### 7. Confidentiality
Unreleased Works and business terms are confidential and shall not be disclosed without consent.

### 8. Representations
Each Collaborator warrants their contributions are original or properly cleared and that they are free to enter this Agreement.

### 9. Governing Law; Disputes
Governed by the laws of [STATE/COUNTRY]. Disputes resolved by [mediation, then arbitration/courts] in [VENUE].

### 10. Entire Agreement; Amendment
This is the entire agreement among the Collaborators and may be amended only in a signed writing.
