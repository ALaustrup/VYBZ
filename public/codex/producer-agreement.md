# Producer Agreement

**A Codex document provided by Astra Matrix, Inc.**

This agreement sets the terms under which a producer delivers production services for a recording — defining ownership of the master, the producer's fee and/or royalty ("points"), any publishing share for co-written material, and credit.

---

## PRODUCER AGREEMENT

This Producer Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of [DATE] between:

- **Artist / Company:** [ARTIST OR LABEL NAME], [ADDRESS] ("Artist"); and
- **Producer:** [PRODUCER LEGAL NAME], p/k/a [PRODUCER NAME], [ADDRESS] ("Producer").

### 1. Services
Producer will produce [NUMBER] master recording(s) of the composition(s) tentatively titled [TITLE(S)] (each a "Master"), including [arranging, programming, recording, editing, and mixing as applicable].

### 2. Compensation
- **(a) Advance / Fee.** Artist shall pay Producer a [non-recoupable / recoupable] fee of **$[AMOUNT]**, payable [50% on signing, 50% on delivery / other].
- **(b) Royalty ("Points").** Artist shall pay Producer a royalty of **[3–5]%** of [net receipts / the applicable royalty base] from exploitation of the Master(s), accounted [semi-annually], subject to recoupment of the fee in (a) where stated.
- **(c) Producer Advances Recoupable From Royalties.** Any advance is recoupable solely from Producer's royalties, not from Artist personally.

### 3. Ownership of Master
The Master(s) and all copyrights therein are the property of **[Artist / Company]** as a work made for hire, or, to the extent not a work for hire, Producer hereby assigns all right, title, and interest in the Master(s) to Artist. Producer retains no ownership of the sound recording except the royalty in Section 2(b).

### 4. Composition / Publishing
If Producer contributes original musical material to the underlying composition, the parties shall execute a **Split Sheet** allocating the composition. Absent a signed Split Sheet, Producer's contribution to the composition is **[0% / as agreed]**.

### 5. Credit
Producer shall receive credit substantially in the form: **"Produced by [PRODUCER NAME]"** on all releases and metadata where reasonably practicable.

### 6. Samples & Clearances
Producer represents that the Master contains no third-party samples or interpolations except as disclosed in **Exhibit A**, and that any disclosed materials are Artist's responsibility to clear unless otherwise agreed.

### 7. Representations & Warranties
Each party represents it has the right to enter this Agreement and that its contributions are original or properly licensed and do not infringe any third-party rights.

### 8. Delivery & Deliverables
Producer shall deliver the final mixed Master(s), plus session files/stems on request, by [DELIVERY DATE].

### 9. Governing Law; Disputes
This Agreement is governed by the laws of [STATE/COUNTRY]. Disputes shall be resolved by [negotiation, then mediation/arbitration in [VENUE] / the courts of [VENUE]].

### 10. Entire Agreement
This Agreement is the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions. It may be amended only in a signed writing.
