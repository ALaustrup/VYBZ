# Artist Management Agreement

**A Codex document provided by Astra Matrix, Inc.**

A management agreement engages a manager to guide and develop an artist's career in exchange for a commission. Key terms: scope, commission rate and base, term and "sunset" (post-term commissions), exclusions, and termination.

---

## ARTIST MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT

**Effective Date:** [DATE]
**Artist:** [ARTIST LEGAL NAME], p/k/a [ARTIST NAME] ("Artist")
**Manager:** [MANAGER / COMPANY NAME] ("Manager")

### 1. Engagement & Scope
Artist engages Manager as Artist's **[exclusive]** personal manager to advise and counsel on Artist's career in the entertainment industry, including recording, live performance, publishing, branding, and team-building. Manager is not an employment agent, booking agent, or attorney and will not act in those capacities where licensure is required.

### 2. Term
The term is **[X] years** beginning on the Effective Date, [with options / renewable by mutual agreement].

### 3. Commission
- **Rate:** Manager shall receive **[15–20]%** of Artist's **Gross Earnings** in the entertainment industry during the term (and post-term per Section 5).
- **Gross Earnings** means [define: earnings from recording, live, publishing, merch, endorsements, etc.].
- **Exclusions:** commission shall **not** apply to [recording/production costs, tour support, sound & lights, opening-act fees, songwriting mechanicals (if excluded), monies paid to third parties, and other pass-through costs] as agreed.

### 4. Expenses
Pre-approved, reasonable expenses incurred on Artist's behalf are reimbursable with documentation; individual expenses over **$[AMOUNT]** require Artist's prior approval.

### 5. Sunset / Post-Term Commissions
On agreements **entered during the term**, Manager shall receive a declining commission after the term: e.g., **[100% for 1 year, 66% for year 2, 33% for year 3, then 0%]**, or as agreed. No commission accrues on agreements entered after the term.

### 6. Authority
Manager may [make recommendations only / with Artist's written approval] enter into agreements or incur obligations on Artist's behalf. Artist retains final approval over material decisions.

### 7. Key Person
Manager's services shall be rendered principally by **[KEY PERSON]**. If the Key Person is unavailable, Artist may terminate on notice.

### 8. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within **[30] days'** notice. Sections on post-term commissions, confidentiality, and accounting survive.

### 9. Confidentiality; Independent Contractor
Manager shall keep Artist's information confidential. Manager is an independent contractor, not an employee or partner of Artist.

### 10. Governing Law
Governed by the laws of [STATE/COUNTRY]; venue [VENUE]. [Note: some jurisdictions regulate talent representation — confirm local rules.]
