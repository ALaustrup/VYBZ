# Featured Artist Agreement

**A Codex document provided by Astra Matrix, Inc.**

When a guest artist ("feature") performs on a track, this agreement fixes their master-recording split, any composition split, credit ("feat."), approvals, and compensation — the details a split sheet alone does not cover.

---

## FEATURED ARTIST AGREEMENT

This Featured Artist Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of [DATE] between **[MAIN ARTIST/LABEL]** ("Artist") and **[FEATURED ARTIST LEGAL NAME]**, p/k/a **[FEATURED NAME]** ("Featured Artist"), regarding the recording tentatively titled **"[TITLE]"** (the "Recording").

### 1. Services
Featured Artist will record a guest performance (vocals/instrumentation) for the Recording, and deliver it by [DATE] in [format/stems].

### 2. Compensation
- **(a)** A one-time fee of **$[AMOUNT]** [and/or]
- **(b)** A master royalty of **[X]%** of net receipts from the Recording, accounted [semi-annually]; and/or
- **(c)** [Other: e.g., 50/50 net split].

### 3. Master Ownership
Ownership of the Recording is **[Artist / split [X]%/[Y]%]**. Except for the compensation in Section 2, Featured Artist grants Artist the right to reproduce, distribute, perform, and license the Recording worldwide in perpetuity.

### 4. Composition Split
If Featured Artist contributes to the underlying composition, the parties shall sign a **Split Sheet**. Absent one, Featured Artist's composition share is **[0% / as agreed]**.

### 5. Credit
Featured Artist shall be credited as **"[MAIN ARTIST] feat. [FEATURED NAME]"** in titles/metadata where practicable.

### 6. Approvals & Reuse
Featured Artist's performance may be used in the Recording and its promotion. Use in **remixes, syncs, or other songs** requires Featured Artist's [prior written consent / is pre-approved].

### 7. Name, Image & Likeness
Featured Artist grants Artist the right to use Featured Artist's approved name, image, and likeness solely to credit and promote the Recording.

### 8. Warranties
Each party warrants it holds the rights necessary for this Agreement and that its contribution is original or cleared.

### 9. Governing Law
Governed by the laws of [STATE/COUNTRY]; disputes resolved in [VENUE].
