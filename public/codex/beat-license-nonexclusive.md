# Non-Exclusive Beat License

**A Codex document provided by Astra Matrix, Inc.**

A non-exclusive beat license lets an artist use a producer's instrumental ("beat") within stated limits, while the producer keeps ownership and may license the same beat to others. Larger caps or exclusivity require a separate agreement.
