# Songwriter Split Sheet

**A Codex document provided by Astra Matrix, Inc.**

A split sheet records who contributed to a musical composition and the percentage of ownership each contributor holds. Executed at the time of creation, it becomes the authoritative record used to register the work and to collect and divide publishing royalties. Ownership percentages must total exactly 100%.

### 2. Agreement of the Parties
Each Writer represents that (a) their contribution is original and does not infringe any third party's rights; (b) the shares above accurately reflect the parties' agreement; and (c) each Writer is authorized to enter into this Agreement. The shares above shall be used to register the Composition with the parties' respective performing rights organizations and any collection society, and to allocate all publishing income.

### 3. Master Recording
This Split Sheet governs the **composition** only. Ownership of any **sound recording (master)** of the Composition is addressed separately in a recording, producer, or featured-artist agreement.
