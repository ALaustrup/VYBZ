# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

**A Codex document provided by Astra Matrix, Inc.**

A mutual NDA protects confidential information both parties share while exploring or working on a project — unreleased music, business terms, contacts, and strategy — so ideas can be exchanged safely.

---

## MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of [DATE] between **[PARTY A NAME]**, [ADDRESS] and **[PARTY B NAME]**, [ADDRESS] (each a "Party," and as recipient, the "Receiving Party," and as discloser, the "Disclosing Party").

### 1. Purpose
The Parties wish to explore and/or pursue [describe: a potential collaboration, release, or business relationship] (the "Purpose") and may disclose confidential information to one another.

### 2. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by a Party, in any form, including unreleased recordings, compositions, stems, session files, business and financial terms, contacts, marketing plans, and anything reasonably understood to be confidential.

### 3. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was known to the Receiving Party without obligation before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information; or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

### 4. Obligations
The Receiving Party shall (a) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (b) not disclose it to third parties except to representatives who need to know and are bound by confidentiality; and (c) protect it with at least reasonable care.

### 5. Compelled Disclosure
If legally compelled to disclose, the Receiving Party shall give prompt notice (where lawful) and disclose only what is required.

### 6. No License; No Obligation
No license or ownership is granted by disclosure. Nothing obligates either Party to proceed with the Purpose.

### 7. Term
Confidentiality obligations continue for **[3] years** from disclosure (or, for unreleased recordings and trade secrets, until publicly released or no longer a trade secret).

### 8. Return/Destruction
Upon request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy Confidential Information and copies.

### 9. Remedies
The Parties agree that breach may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages are inadequate, and the Disclosing Party may seek injunctive relief in addition to other remedies.

### 10. Governing Law
Governed by the laws of [STATE/COUNTRY]. Venue: [VENUE].
