# Cease & Desist Letter (Music Infringement)

**A Codex document provided by Astra Matrix, Inc.**

A cease-and-desist letter formally demands that another party stop conduct that infringes your rights — for example, unauthorized use of your recording, composition, name, or artwork — and creates a dated record before any further action.

---

## CEASE AND DESIST LETTER

**Date:** [DATE]
**Via:** [Email and/or Certified Mail]

**To:** [RECIPIENT NAME / ENTITY], [ADDRESS / EMAIL]
**From:** [YOUR NAME / ENTITY], [ADDRESS / EMAIL]

**Re: Demand to Cease and Desist Unauthorized Use of [WORK/NAME]**

Dear [RECIPIENT]:

I am the owner of the rights in the following work / mark (the "Property"): **[describe — e.g., the sound recording and composition "[TITLE]"; the artist name/mark "[NAME]"; the artwork "[DESCRIPTION]"]**, [registration/ISRC/UPC or first-use details if any].

It has come to my attention that you are using the Property without authorization, specifically: **[describe the infringing conduct — e.g., distributing "[TITLE]" on [platform/URL]; using the name "[NAME]"; posting the artwork at [URL]]**, first observed on or about **[DATE]**. This use is unauthorized and infringes my rights under applicable copyright, trademark, and/or related law.

**Demand.** I demand that you immediately:
1. **Cease and desist** all use of the Property;
2. **Remove** all infringing content from [platforms/URLs] within **[10] days** of this letter;
3. **Refrain** from any further use of the Property; and
4. Provide **written confirmation** of your compliance and an accounting of any revenue derived from the infringing use.

This letter is not a complete statement of the facts or law and does not waive any rights or remedies, all of which are expressly reserved. If you do not comply by **[DEADLINE]**, I may pursue all available remedies, including [a DMCA takedown, statutory damages, injunctive relief, and recovery of attorneys' fees where available].

Please direct any response to **[CONTACT]**. I would prefer to resolve this matter promptly and without further action.

Sincerely,

_________________________
[YOUR NAME], [TITLE/ENTITY]
